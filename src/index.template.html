Why Mintr

Built for people who check first

Numbers up front

Price, daily move, volume and depth sit side by side for every row. Nothing is hidden behind a click.

Nothing guessed

If a fact has not been confirmed, the page says it is pending instead of dressing it up. What you see is what is known.

Fast on Solana

Cheap, quick settlement means a token can be found, judged and moved in minutes. The board keeps pace with it.

How it works

Three moves, in order

Survey

Scan the board and sort it three ways: by volume, by biggest movers, or by how deep the liquidity runs.

Verify

Copy a token address with one tap and check it against the explorer you trust before anything else.

Decide

Act on your own read. Mintr shows the ground; it never tells you where to swing.
