# Mintr Whitepaper

Draft. Figures marked TBD are placeholders until they are confirmed.

## Vision

A market is easier to trust when the numbers are in plain view. Mintr is a Solana token that treats its own website as the first proof: a live board of what the chain is actually trading, with every unconfirmed fact labelled as pending instead of filled in with a guess.

## Mission

- Show real activity, ranked by what people traded, without decoration that hides it.
- Say "pending" when something is not confirmed, and publish it the moment it is.
- Keep the tooling open so the community can check the work.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Surface
Website, social presence and the live market board go public.

### Phase 2: Launch
The contract address is announced, pinned to the top of the site, and verifiable on a public explorer.

### Phase 3: Tunnel
Open-source repository, community-run data views and public documentation.

### Phase 4: Depth
Longer-term utility, scoped openly with holders once the earlier phases have shipped.
